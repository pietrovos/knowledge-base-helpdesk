# Chargebacks (internal)

A chargeback is a dispute filed by the customer with their bank. Once a chargeback is open, do not issue a refund for the same charge: the bank reverses the payment itself, and a refund would pay the customer twice.

Forward all chargeback notifications to the Billing Team queue within 1 business day. The billing lead submits evidence (order details, tracking, customer emails) through the payment processor within 7 days of the notification.

If a customer says they plan to file a chargeback, the agent should resolve the underlying issue first and explain that a refund is faster than a dispute (5 business days versus up to 75 days).
