# Duplicate and Unexpected Charges (internal)

## Pending authorizations vs. real charges

When a customer reports being charged twice, first check the payment record in the billing console. A second line marked "authorization" is a temporary hold, not a charge: it drops off the customer's statement automatically within 3 to 5 business days and no action is needed.

## Confirmed duplicate charges

If two captured charges exist for the same order or subscription renewal, refund the duplicate capture from the billing console (Payments > Refund) immediately. Select the reason "Duplicate charge". The customer receives an automatic email and the refund appears within 5 to 10 business days depending on their bank.

## Goodwill credits

For a confirmed duplicate charge, agents on the Billing Team may add a goodwill credit of up to $10 to the customer's account. Credits above $10 need approval from a billing lead. Always add an internal note explaining the reason for the credit.

## Subscription charged after cancellation

If a Kestrel Cloud renewal was charged after the customer cancelled, check the cancellation timestamp. If the cancellation happened before the renewal date, refund the renewal in full. If it happened after the renewal, the normal subscription refund rules apply.
