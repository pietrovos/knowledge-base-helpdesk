# Accounts and Privacy

## Password resets

Customers reset their password from the sign-in page with "Forgot password". The reset link is valid for 30 minutes and can be used once. If the email doesn't arrive within 10 minutes, the customer should check spam and confirm the address; agents can trigger a new link from the admin console but never set a password on a customer's behalf.

## Two-step verification

Customers can enable two-step verification with an authenticator app under Account > Security. If a customer loses access to their authenticator, they can use one of the 10 backup codes generated at setup. Agents cannot disable two-step verification; the customer must complete identity verification through the account recovery form, which takes up to 3 business days.

## Changing the account email

The account email can be changed under Account > Profile. A confirmation link is sent to the new address and the change only takes effect once it is confirmed.

## Deleting an account

Customers can request account deletion under Account > Privacy > Delete account. Deletion is completed within 30 days. Order records are kept for 7 years for tax purposes, but they are disconnected from the deleted profile. Active Kestrel Cloud subscriptions must be cancelled before deletion.

## Data export

Customers can download a copy of their personal data under Account > Privacy > Export my data. The export is prepared within 48 hours and the download link is emailed to the account address.
