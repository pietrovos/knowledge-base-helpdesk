# Returns and Refunds

Kestrel Audio accepts returns of hardware purchased directly from kestrelaudio.com. Products bought from other retailers must be returned to that retailer.

## Return window

Customers can return any hardware product within 30 days of delivery for a full refund. The item must include all original accessories (cables, ear tips, charging case and carry pouch).

Opened earbuds and in-ear products can be returned within the same 30 days, but the ear tips must be removed and discarded for hygiene reasons. We include a fresh set of ear tips with every replacement.

After 30 days, hardware can no longer be returned for a refund. Faulty items are handled under the warranty instead (see the Warranty policy).

## How to start a return

Customers start a return from Account > Orders > Request return. They receive a prepaid return label by email within 1 business day. Return shipping is free within the United States and Canada.

Customers outside the United States and Canada pay their own return shipping and should use a tracked service. We are not responsible for returns lost in transit without tracking.

## Refund timing

Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5 business days after the returned item arrives at our warehouse and passes inspection. Inspection usually takes 2 business days after delivery to the warehouse.

Banks can take an additional 3 to 10 business days to show the refund on a card statement. If a customer has waited more than 15 business days after our refund confirmation email, an agent should escalate to the Billing Team with the order number.

## Items that cannot be refunded

Gift cards are non-refundable and cannot be exchanged for cash, except where required by law.

Items marked "Final sale" (open-box and clearance items) cannot be returned or refunded.

Kestrel Cloud subscriptions follow their own cancellation rules (see Kestrel Cloud plans and billing).

## Exchanges

We don't offer direct exchanges. Customers who want a different color or model should return the original item for a refund and place a new order.
