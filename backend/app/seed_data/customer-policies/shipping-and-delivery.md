# Shipping and Delivery

## Shipping options and costs

| Method | Delivery time | Cost |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Standard | 3–5 business days | Free on orders over $50, otherwise $6.95 |
| Express | 1–2 business days | $14.95 |
| International | 7–14 business days | Calculated at checkout |

Orders placed before 2 pm Eastern Time on a business day ship the same day. Orders placed later, or on weekends and US holidays, ship the next business day.

## International orders

We ship to the United States, Canada, the United Kingdom, the European Union and Australia. We don't currently ship to other countries.

International customers are responsible for import duties and taxes, which are collected by the carrier on delivery. Packages refused because of unpaid duties are not eligible for a refund of the original shipping cost.

## Changing or cancelling an order

Customers can change the shipping address or cancel an order within 1 hour of placing it, from Account > Orders. After that the order is already being packed and can't be changed; the customer can return it after delivery instead.

## Lost, late or damaged packages

If tracking shows no movement for 5 business days, or a package is marked delivered but wasn't received, the customer should contact us. Agents open a carrier claim and ship a replacement once the claim is filed, without waiting for the carrier's decision, for orders under $300.

For orders of $300 or more, the replacement ships once the carrier confirms the loss, which usually takes up to 10 business days.

Packages that arrive damaged should be photographed before unpacking further. Agents should request photos of the box and the item, then ship a replacement at no cost.
