# Limited Warranty

## Coverage

All Kestrel Audio hardware comes with a 2-year limited warranty from the date of delivery. The warranty covers defects in materials and workmanship under normal use.

Battery capacity is covered: if a battery holds less than 70% of its original capacity within the 2-year warranty period, the product qualifies for a warranty replacement.

## What the warranty does not cover

The warranty does not cover accidental damage (drops, cracked housings, crushed cables), water damage beyond the product's rated water resistance, normal cosmetic wear, lost ear tips or charging cases, or damage caused by unauthorized repair.

Products bought second-hand or from unauthorized sellers are not covered.

## Making a warranty claim

To file a claim, the customer needs the product's serial number (printed inside the charging case lid or under the headband padding) and proof of purchase. Agents create the claim in the warranty portal and attach both.

Approved claims are fulfilled with a replacement of the same model, or the closest current equivalent if the model has been discontinued. We don't repair units and we don't issue refunds for warranty claims.

The replacement unit is covered for the remainder of the original warranty period or 90 days, whichever is longer.

## Lost single earbuds

A single lost earbud is not covered by the warranty, but customers can buy a replacement earbud for $39 from Account > Support > Replacement parts.
