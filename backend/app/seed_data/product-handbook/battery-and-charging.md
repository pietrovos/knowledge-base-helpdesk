# Battery and Charging

## Battery life

| Product | Listening time | With charging case |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Kestrel Buds | 8 hours | 32 hours |
| Kestrel Buds Pro | 7 hours (6 with noise cancelling) | 28 hours |
| Kestrel Over-Ear | 40 hours (30 with noise cancelling) | n/a |

## Charging

All current products charge over USB-C. A 10-minute quick charge gives about 2 hours of listening. The charging cases also support Qi wireless charging.

Use a USB-C charger rated 5V/2A or higher. We don't recommend charging from computer USB ports that are in sleep mode, because they can cut power intermittently.

## Battery health

Batteries naturally lose capacity over time. To get the best lifespan, avoid storing products fully discharged for long periods and keep them away from heat above 35°C (95°F).

Battery capacity below 70% within 2 years of delivery is covered by the warranty.
