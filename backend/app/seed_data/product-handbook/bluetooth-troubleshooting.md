# Bluetooth and Connection Troubleshooting

## Pairing a new device

1. Open the charging case with both earbuds inside (or hold the headphones' power button for 5 seconds).
2. Wait for the status light to flash white: the product is in pairing mode.
3. Select the product in the phone or computer's Bluetooth settings.

## Connection drops

Most dropouts come from interference or distance. Ask the customer to keep the source device within 10 meters with no walls in between, and to turn off Wi-Fi on the phone briefly to test.

If dropouts continue, update the firmware from the Kestrel app (Device > Firmware). Firmware 4.2 and later fixed dropouts with some Android 15 phones.

## Factory reset

A factory reset clears all pairings. For earbuds: place both earbuds in the case, keep the lid open, and hold the case button for 15 seconds until the light flashes amber three times. For headphones: hold the power and volume-down buttons together for 10 seconds.

After a reset, the customer must remove the product from their device's Bluetooth list before pairing again.

## Multipoint

Pro-series headphones can stay connected to two devices at once (multipoint). Multipoint is enabled in the Kestrel app under Device > Connections. Earbuds don't support multipoint.

## One earbud not playing

Clean the charging contacts on the earbud and inside the case with a dry cotton swab, then charge for 30 minutes. If one earbud still doesn't play, do a factory reset. If the problem persists after the reset, it's a warranty case.
