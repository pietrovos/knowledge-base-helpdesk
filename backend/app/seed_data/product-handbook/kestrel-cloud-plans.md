# Kestrel Cloud Plans and Billing

Kestrel Cloud is the subscription service behind the Kestrel app: custom EQ profiles, firmware early access, spatial audio and multi-device sync.

## Plans

| Plan | Price | Devices | Highlights |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Free | $0 | 1 | Basic EQ presets, firmware updates |
| Plus | $4.99 per month | 3 | Custom EQ, multi-device sync |
| Pro | $9.99 per month or $99 per year | 10 | Everything in Plus, spatial audio, early-access firmware, priority support |

## Free trial

New accounts get a 14-day free trial of Pro. A payment method is required to start the trial and the subscription renews automatically as monthly Pro unless it is cancelled before the trial ends.

## Upgrades and downgrades

Upgrades take effect immediately and the price difference is prorated for the rest of the billing period. Downgrades take effect at the start of the next billing period.

## Cancellation and refunds

Customers can cancel at any time under Account > Subscription. After cancelling, the plan stays active until the end of the period already paid for, then the account moves to Free.

Monthly subscriptions are not refunded for partial months. Annual Pro subscriptions can be refunded in full within 14 days of the purchase or renewal date; after 14 days, annual plans are not refundable.

## Failed payments

If a renewal payment fails, we retry the card 3 times over 7 days and email the customer each time. If all retries fail, the account moves to Free and custom EQ profiles are kept for 90 days.
