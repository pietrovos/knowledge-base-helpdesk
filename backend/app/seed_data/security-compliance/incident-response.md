# Security Incident Response (restricted)

## Reporting

Any suspected account takeover, data exposure or phishing campaign impersonating Kestrel Audio must be reported in the #sec-incidents channel and to security@kestrelaudio.com immediately. Do not discuss suspected incidents with customers beyond confirming that we are investigating.

## Account takeover

When a customer reports an unauthorized login, lock the account from the admin console (Security > Lock account), revoke all sessions and require a password reset. Security reviews the login history within 4 hours.

## Customer notification

Only the security lead decides whether customers must be notified of an incident. The regulatory notification deadline is 72 hours from discovery for EU customers.
